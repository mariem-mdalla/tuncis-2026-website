Imed Boughzala 
Imed Boughzala is General Director (Dean) of ENSIIE, France, and President of TunAISa. A Distinguished Professor of Information Systems, HDR-accredited in Computer and Management Sciences, his research focuses on digital intelligence, digital transformation, and data governance. He previously served as Dean of Faculty at IMT-BS, where he founded the SMART² research group.


Narjès Bellamine Ben Saoud
Narjès Bellamine Ben Saoud is Director of ENSI (École Nationale des Sciences de l'Informatique) and Director of the RIADI Laboratory, University of Manouba, Tunisia. A Professor in the Department of Information Systems and Decision Engineering, she teaches Software Engineering and Artificial Intelligence. She holds an engineering diploma from ENSEEIHT Toulouse, a PhD from the University of Toulouse 1, and an HDR from ENSI. Her research focuses on modeling and simulation of complex socio-technical systems, information systems, and multi-agent systems. She was also a Fulbright scholar at the University of California, Irvine.


Takoua Abdellatif 
Takoua Abdellatif is Associate Professor at ENISo (National School of Engineers of Sousse), University of Sousse, and a researcher at the SERCOM Laboratory, Polytechnic School of Tunisia, University of Carthage. She holds an engineering degree from ENSIMAG (Grenoble, France), a PhD from Grenoble University, and an HDR from the University of Carthage. Her research focuses on Big Data, IoT, and Cloud Computing. She is also a co-founder of the Horizon School of Digital Technologies in Sousse, and an NVIDIA Ambassador in Generative AI.
