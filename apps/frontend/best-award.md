Best Paper Award

TUNCIS2026 will present a Best Paper Award to recognize outstanding research contributions presented at the conference.

The award includes:

An official award certificate, signed and stamped by AIS (Association for Information Systems)
Public announcement of the winning paper during the conference
Recognition on TUNCIS2026's official social media channels

Selection: The winning paper will be selected by the Scientific Committee based on originality, scientific rigor, and relevance to the conference themes.
